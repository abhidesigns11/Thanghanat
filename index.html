What is Thanganat?

Thanganat (થનગનાટ) means the joyous, restless flutter of the heart before dancing. It is South Gujarat’s premier cultural and philanthropic Navratri festival, hosted in Vapi, Gujarat, under the stewardship of the Rotary Club of Vapi (Rotary International District 3060).

The core distinction: unlike commercial Garba events where profits go to event management firms, Thanganat is 100% philanthropic. Every rupee generated through visitor passes, VIP lounges, exhibitor stalls and corporate sponsorships directly funds life-saving medical procedures at Haria L.G. Rotary Hospital in Vapi.

The sacred cause

Garba with a heart

The soul of Thanganat lies in its direct social impact.

Haria L.G. Rotary Hospital is a renowned multi-specialty charitable hospital established by the Rotary Club of Vapi and Rotary Charitable Trust — the beneficiary of the festival.

Mission 01

Free Dialysis Center

Life-saving, zero-cost renal dialysis sessions for underprivileged patients suffering from end-stage kidney failure.

Mission 02

“Little Hearts” Initiative

Sponsoring pediatric cardiac surgeries for infants and children born with congenital heart defects from economically weaker families who cannot afford multi-lakh hospital procedures.

Reach

1,000+ direct beneficiaries

Supported annually solely from festival proceeds.

    Guidelines, safety & FAQs

    Before you step in

    Attire

    Strict traditional attire is required for the central dancing floor — Chaniya Choli / Kediyu / Kurta-Dhoti.

    Entry

    Mandatory physical or digital pass scanning at RFID/barcode turnstiles.

    Zero tolerance

    Alcohol, tobacco and unauthorized items are strictly banned; round-the-clock police and private bouncer patrol.
