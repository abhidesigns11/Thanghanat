In short

This website does not use cookies, local storage or similar tracking technology. There are no analytics, advertising, social-media pixels or embedded third-party videos, maps or widgets. So there is no cookie banner and nothing for you to accept or reject.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar tools, such as local storage, do the same job. They are often used to remember you, measure visits or show ads.

2. What this site uses

Cookies we set
None.
Local or session storage in your browser
None. The site does not save anything on your device.
Analytics or visitor tracking
None.
Advertising or social-media trackers
None.
Embedded third-party content
None. All photos and videos are served from this site itself.

3. Third-party requests

Google Fonts
The site's fonts load from Google. Your browser asks Google servers for the font files, so Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google states that the Fonts service does not set cookies. Google's own privacy policy applies to that request.
Links you choose to click
WhatsApp, Instagram and phone links leave this site. Those services may set their own cookies once you open them. We do not control that.

4. Do we need your consent?

No. Because we do not use cookies or tracking technology, there is nothing to ask consent for. If we add any in future, we will first update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Your privacy rights are in the Privacy Policy.

5. Your browser controls

You can block or delete cookies and block third-party requests in your browser settings. You can also use a content blocker to stop the Google Fonts request. The site will still work, but your device's default fonts will be used.
