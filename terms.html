In short

This site gives information about Thanganat 2026. You cannot buy passes on it. Passes, stalls and sponsorships are arranged through the pass office or the helpdesk. By using the site you agree to these terms.

1. About these terms

These terms apply to your use of this website and to your attendance at Thanganat 2026 — Rotary Vapi Navratri Mahotsav, organised by the Rotary Club of Vapi (Rotary International District 3060), 11–19 October 2026 at ROFEL College Ground, GIDC, Vapi, Gujarat, India. "We" means the Rotary Club of Vapi as organiser. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

2. Information on this site

We try to keep the site accurate, but the schedule, artists, venue arrangements, passes and prices can change. Please call the helpdesk (+91 90997 99922 or +91 96248 80492) to confirm before you rely on anything, especially for travel or spending. Nothing on the site is an offer that binds us to sell you a pass or stall until it is confirmed by the pass office.

3. Passes and entry

  • Passes are sold at the ROFEL College Office (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM) and as confirmed by the helpdesk. This website does not sell passes or take payments.
  • You need a valid pass for the night and area you enter. Passes may be checked at the gates.
  • The dress code for the central dance floor is traditional attire.
  • Alcohol, tobacco and unauthorised items are not allowed inside the ground.
  • We may refuse entry to, or ask to leave, anyone who breaks these rules, puts others at risk, or behaves in a way that spoils the event for others.
  • Refunds are covered in the Refund Policy.

4. Your safety and belongings

Please follow the instructions of volunteers and security staff, use the marked exits, and look after your children and belongings. Dancing involves movement in a crowd; take reasonable care of yourself and others.

5. Photography at the event

Thanganat is a public event and the organisers and media partners may take photos and videos that include the crowd. See the Privacy Policy for how we use them and how to ask for removal.

6. Competitions and awards

Winners are chosen by the judges using the criteria shown on the site, and the judges' decision is final. Prizes and trophies are as announced by the organisers. A parent or guardian must enter and consent for any child taking part.

7. Stalls and sponsorships

Stall and sponsorship enquiries are handled by phone or WhatsApp. Prices on the site are indicative. The final price, any taxes, what is included and the payment terms are confirmed in writing when you book.

8. Charitable purpose

Thanganat is organised as a charitable fundraising festival. Funds raised are intended to support the work of Haria L.G. Rotary Hospital in Vapi. Payments for passes, stalls and sponsorships are payments for entry or participation. They are not donations unless we say so in writing, and we do not promise any tax benefit.
