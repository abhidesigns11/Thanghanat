1. Who we are

This website is for Thanganat 2026 — Rotary Vapi Navratri Mahotsav, organised by the Rotary Club of Vapi (Rotary International District 3060) at ROFEL College Ground, GIDC, Vapi, Gujarat, India. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean the Rotary Club of Vapi acting as organiser. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described below.

Contact: +91 90997 99922 or +91 96248 80492. Write to: Thanganat 2026 helpdesk, Rotary Club of Vapi, ROFEL College Office, GIDC Campus, Vapi, Gujarat, India (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM).

2. What we collect, why, and how

Visiting this website
We do not collect, store or track anything about you through the site. Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs (for example IP address, browser type and time of visit) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.
Fonts from Google
The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore contacts Google servers, and Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google states that the Fonts service does not set cookies. See Cookie Policy.
Phone calls
If you call the helpdesk we may note your name, phone number and what you asked, so we can answer you. Purpose: answer your question about passes, stalls, sponsorship or the event.
WhatsApp messages
The "Enquire on WhatsApp" button opens WhatsApp. If you send a message we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name and your message. WhatsApp (Meta) also handles that data under its own privacy policy. Purpose: reply to your enquiry.
Pass office and stall bookings
Passes are sold at the ROFEL College Office and stalls are booked by enquiry, not through this website. If you buy a pass or book a stall we collect only what is needed to issue the pass or confirm the booking, for example your name, phone number, the pass or stall type, and payment details for our accounts. This website does not take payments and never asks for card or bank details.
Photos and videos at the ground
See section 8.

We do not ask for sensitive data such as ID numbers, health, religion or location, and we ask you not to send it to us.

3. Why we may use your data (legal basis)

We use personal data only for the specific purpose for which you gave it to us — for example, to answer your question or issue your pass. Where you voluntarily give us your data for a specified purpose and have not told us you object, the DPDP Act allows us to use it for that purpose (Section 7). If we ever want to use data for something else, we will tell you first and ask for your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us; withdrawing is as easy as giving it.

4. Who we share it with

  • Our volunteers and office staff who need it to deal with your request.
  • Service providers we rely on, such as WhatsApp (for messages), our website host and, if you pay, our bank or payment provider.
  • Authorities, courts or others where the law requires it.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We do not transfer your data outside India except where a service you choose (such as WhatsApp) does so under its own terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to answer you and to run Thanganat 2026. We aim to delete them within 90 days after the festival ends (19 October 2026). Records we must keep by law, such as accounts and receipts for pass or stall payments, are kept for the period the law requires and then deleted.

6. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data, such as limiting access to those who need it and keeping paper and phone records secure. No method is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold, why, and who we shared it with;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it (unless we must keep it by law);
  • deal with a complaint (grievance redressal); and
  • let you nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot do so yourself.

How to ask: call +91 90997 99922 or +91 96248 80492 or write to Thanganat 2026 helpdesk, Rotary Club of Vapi, ROFEL College Office, GIDC Campus, Vapi, Gujarat, India (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM). Please tell us your name, the phone number you used, and what you want. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act. We aim to reply within 30 days, and in any case within the time the law allows.

Grievance contact: The Grievance Contact, Thanganat 2026 Organising Committee, Rotary Club of Vapi, at the address above. If we do not resolve your complaint, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act provides.

8. Photos and videos at the festival

Thanganat is a public event. The organisers and media partners may take photos and videos of the ground and the crowd, and we may publish some on this website and our Instagram page (@thanganatbyrotaryvapi (opens in a new tab)). We use them to show the festival, not to identify or track anyone.

If you appear in a photo or video on our website or Instagram and want it removed, contact us with enough detail to find it (for example the photo or reel on the gallery) and we will remove it from our channels promptly. If an identifiable child appears, a parent or guardian can ask us to remove it, and we will do so promptly.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without the verifiable consent of a parent or guardian, as the DPDP Act requires. We do not track children, monitor their behaviour or show them targeted advertising. For the Prince and Princess of Thanganat award (children under 14), a parent or guardian gives the child's details and consent at the pass office.

10. Links to other sites

The site links to WhatsApp, Instagram and your phone's dialler. We do not control those services and they have their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if what we do changes, and change the "Last updated" date. If we add anything that uses cookies or tracking, we will first update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
