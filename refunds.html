In short

Passes are not refundable once issued, except in the cases below: the festival or a night is cancelled by the organisers, or we made a mistake. This website does not sell passes. They are bought at the pass office, so refunds are also handled there.

1. What this covers

This policy covers passes and stall or sponsorship payments for Thanganat 2026 (11–19 October 2026, ROFEL College Ground, GIDC, Vapi) organised by the Rotary Club of Vapi. Because this website takes no payments, there is nothing to cancel online.

2. When you can get a refund

The whole festival is cancelled before it begins
Full refund of the amount you paid.
A night is cancelled before it starts
You get back the share of your price for that night. For a season pass, that is the price paid divided by the number of nights the pass covers. For a single-night pass, it is the full price.
A night is stopped after it has started
Handled case by case. Contact the helpdesk.
We made a mistake
If you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or given the wrong pass because of our error, we will refund the difference or replace the pass.
Stall or sponsorship payments
As set out in your written booking. If the organisers cancel the festival or a night, the part of your fee for the cancelled night(s) is refunded in the same way as passes.

3. When a refund is not available

  • You cannot attend, change your plans, or arrive late.
  • Entry is refused or you are asked to leave because you broke the rules (for example dress code, prohibited items or unsafe behaviour). See the Terms & Conditions.
  • A pass is lost, damaged or used by someone else. Ask the helpdesk what can be done.

4. How to ask for a refund

  1. Call +91 90997 99922 or +91 96248 80492 or visit the pass office: ROFEL College Office, GIDC Campus, Vapi (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM).
  2. Bring your pass and receipt, and tell us your name and phone number.
  3. Ask within 7 days of the cancelled night, or of the mistake.

We aim to pay approved refunds within 15 working days, by the same method you paid with, or another method we agree with you.

5. Your legal rights

Nothing here limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or other Indian law.

6. Contact
